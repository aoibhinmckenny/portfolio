Inishbofin

Video Project

I filmed and edited a video project capturing the scenery and life of Inishbofin as part of a Multimedia Technology module.

Inishbofin is a small island off the coast of Connemara, County Galway, with a population of around 180 inhabitants. The goal of this piece was to create a relaxing, atmospheric video based on a trip to Inishbofin to showcase the beauty of the island. I wanted to capture the Island’s peaceful nature and highlight the natural beauty of its landscapes.

I shot all the video footage and images used in the project. I used Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects to edit the video. The majority of the ambient sounds used in the video were recorded by myself on the island including waves crashing, wind and a camera shutter.
